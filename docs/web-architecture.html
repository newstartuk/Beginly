Beginly — architecture & current-state reference

How the two eras of this codebase actually fit together right now — verified against the source, not assumed. Pairs with the implementation roadmap; this is the map, that is the plan.

Two eras, one codebase

The working project is the checklist-era product (built and rebranded this session). The v1.3 platform archive merge added a second, larger product surface on top of it. They currently coexist rather than being unified.

Checklist era · original 16 pages

Shell: components/Navigation.tsx (sidebar)

  • dashboard, checklist, tasks/[id], budget, guides, settings, support, bank, emergency, nhs, document-helper
  • admin, admin/guides, admin/tasks
Platform era · merged from v1.3 17 pages

Shell: components/platform/PlatformShell.tsx

  • journey, household(+invitations), opportunities, products(+[id], workspace), nia, referrals, notifications, cities(+[city]), evidence-room
  • partners/commission-readiness, platform
  • admin/operations, admin/observability
⚠Admin is split across both shells. app/admin/page.tsx, admin/guides, admin/tasks use Navigation.tsx; admin/operations and admin/observability use PlatformShell. Same section of the product, two different navigation chromes — this is Roadmap Phase 2's job to resolve.

A third pattern exists for fully public pages — /, /login, /signup hand-roll their own header markup rather than using any shared shell component.
