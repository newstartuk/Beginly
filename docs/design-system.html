Beginly — design system reference

The canonical, currently-implemented system — pulled directly from globals.css and the components built this session, not aspirational. Pairs with the architecture reference.

⚠ Two systems currently coexist in globals.css. The tokens and components below are canonical — used by the landing page, login/signup, and all checklist-era pages. A second, ~48KB hardcoded-hex CSS block (.platform-*, .journey-*, .public-* …) was ported wholesale from the v1.3 merge to make the new platform pages render, and has not been reconciled against these tokens yet — that reconciliation is Roadmap Phase 2, not done here.

Brand identity

Archetype: Caregiver (primary) + Sage (secondary) — protective and warm, backed by cited sources and plain-English trust, not triumphalist or magical.

Caregiver

Service-first, protective. Scam alerts prominent, never fear-mongering. "We're with you."

Sage

Credibility register. Cited sources, plain-English decoding, AI clearly labelled.

Positioning: "Beginly turns overwhelming, scattered settlement information into a single, personalised 90-day plan — tailored to your city, situation and arrival date, telling you plainly what to avoid."

Logo — Stepping Stones

Four dots growing in size, muted grey through teal to gold, connected by a rising line — the last stone haloed as "current." Chosen over five other concepts specifically because it survives scaling to favicon size unmodified (no curves to blur) and echoes the app's own stage-tracker UI.

components/Logo.tsx — props: size (px, default 36), animated (one-time entrance sequence, respects prefers-reduced-motion), className.

Usage: navy for headings/trust anchors, teal for primary actions, gold reserved for progress/premium signals (the readiness score, the "current" stage), aqua for informational accents. Never convey status by colour alone — every colour pairs with an icon or label.

Typography

Display — Lexend, weight 700
Your UK settlement journey, sorted into one plan.
Body — Inter, weight 400
Beginly gives you a personalised 90-day checklist, plain-English guidance, and scam alerts — all built around your arrival. Chosen for this pairing because Lexend was the top match for government/healthcare/finance/accessibility-focused products; Inter stays for body copy since it was already the working font and is functionally interchangeable with the evidence-matched Source Sans pairing.

Motion principles

Restraint is the brand signal, not an afterthought — the matched "Accessible & Ethical" archetype explicitly lists decorative motion as an anti-pattern. Functional motion (feedback, entrance, progress) is on-brand; ambient/looping/parallax motion is not.

fast: 140ms
base: 220ms
slow: 420ms
ease: cubic-bezier(.16, .8, .3, 1)

Applied: hero entrance stagger (0.12s per element), the self-playing roadmap card (2-state loop, functional not decorative — demonstrates the product rather than decorating the page), scroll reveals (once per element, 20–30% viewport threshold). Everything respects prefers-reduced-motion by disabling animation and showing the final state immediately.
